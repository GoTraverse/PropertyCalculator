> equitysight.app uses minimal storage technologies. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking. This page explains exactly what we store and why.

## What we use instead of cookies

equitysight.app primarily uses browser **localStorage** rather than traditional HTTP cookies. localStorage stores data directly on your device and is never automatically sent to our servers with each request, unlike cookies.

We may also use **sessionStorage** for temporary in-session state. This is cleared automatically when you close your browser tab.

## What we store locally

| Key | Purpose | Type | Expires |
|-----|---------|------|---------|
| `propCalc_session_v1` | Stores your login session token so you stay signed in between visits. | Essential | 30 days or on sign-out |
| `propCalc_profile_v1_*` | Stores your display name, avatar colour, and profile preferences. | Functional | Until account deletion |
| `propCalc_scenarios_*` | Cached property scenarios for offline access and faster loading. | Functional | Until cleared or replaced |

## Traditional cookies

We do not set any traditional HTTP cookies for tracking or advertising purposes. Our hosting infrastructure (Netlify) may set technical cookies required for CDN and security functions. These are outside our control and governed by Netlify's own privacy and cookie policies.

## Analytics

We may use privacy-focused analytics tools to understand aggregate usage patterns (e.g. which features are most used). These tools are configured to:

- Not track individuals across sites.
- Anonymise or hash IP addresses.
- Not share data with advertising networks.

## Managing storage

You can clear all locally stored data at any time:

- **In your browser:** Go to Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data → Cached images and files / Site data.
- **In the app:** Sign out from your account — this removes your session token.
- **Full deletion:** Contact us at [support@equitysight.app](mailto:support@equitysight.app) to delete your entire account and all associated data.

## Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy as our practices change. Material changes will be communicated via the Service. See our [Privacy Policy](privacy.html) for broader information on how we handle your data.
