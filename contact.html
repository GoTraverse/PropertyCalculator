EquitySight models the government's equity contribution based on the scheme rules for your state (QLD, NSW, or VIC). It calculates the government's percentage share, what you'll owe when you sell or buy out, and how that changes over time as property values grow. It uses the purchase price, your savings, and the applicable income/property caps to determine eligibility.
Our calculations are based on publicly available scheme rules and standard mortgage calculations. They're designed to give you an accurate planning picture, not a legal quote.
