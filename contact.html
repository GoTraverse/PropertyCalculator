Account problems

I can't log in

Use "Forgot password?" on the login page, or ask for an emailed sign-in link there. If neither works, send me a message with the form above and choose "General question".

I subscribed to Pro before it was withdrawn and want to cancel

Go to your account page, open Subscription and choose "Manage billing". That opens the billing portal, where you can cancel. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. Fees already paid are not refunded for part of a billing period, except where Australian Consumer Law requires it. If something has gone wrong with a payment, send me a message and choose "Billing / subscription".
