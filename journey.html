About the First Home Journey

The First Home Journey splits buying a first home into seven steps. Each one works out its figures from your answers to step 1.

The seven steps

  1. Your details: questions about where you're buying, what you earn, what you've saved and how far along you are. About two minutes.
  2. Compare the schemes: the 5% Deposit Scheme and Help to Buy next to a 20% deposit or 10% with LMI, with when you could buy, the cash needed, the LMI and the repayment for each. Price and income caps are checked against your answers.
  3. Set your budget: the upfront costs including conveyancing, my estimate of what a lender would approve on your income, and your maximum price.
  4. Where to buy: suburbs in your state whose sale median fits your price band, where the state government publishes one (Victoria and metro Adelaide), and a search across 1,473 suburbs that have a government rent or sale figure.
  5. Inspections and offers: a list of the places you look at, bid on or miss out on, each checked against your maximum price.
  6. Contract deadlines: cooling-off, building and pest, finance approval, settlement and your pre-approval expiry, with the days left on each.
  7. Settlement: the final checklist, and what you paid next to the estimates.

Questions

Do I need an account?
No. Without one, your progress is saved in your browser. A free account keeps it on all your devices and lets you share it.
Which government schemes does it compare?
The 5% Deposit Scheme (the expanded First Home Guarantee), the federal Help to Buy shared equity scheme and your state's first home stamp duty concession, next to buying without a scheme with a 20% deposit, or sooner with 10% and lenders mortgage insurance. The figures come from your answers. Confirm eligibility with the scheme administrator and your lender.
Where does the suburb data come from?
State government open data, licensed CC BY 4.0: sale medians from the Valuer-General of Victoria and the Valuer-General of South Australia (metro Adelaide), and median rents from the Queensland RTA, SA Consumer & Business Services and Tasmanian rental bonds. Each figure shows its period and source, and rents are never shown as prices. Other states aren't covered.
Do I have to start at the beginning?
Only for step 1, because the other steps use its answers. When you finish it, the journey opens at the step that matches how far along you are. The earlier steps stay open.
Can my partner or family follow along?
Yes, if you have a free account. A follow-along link lets anyone view your journey, and a partner link lets a signed-in partner edit it with you. Changes show up for the other person the next time they open the page. Anyone with a link can see everything in the journey, including your income and savings. You can turn a link off at any time.
Is this financial advice?
No, it's general information. Each step states its assumptions and uses the same formulas as the calculators on this site, but nothing here takes your circumstances into account. Talk to a licensed adviser, a broker or your lender before you act.

Each calculation also has its own page among the calculators, such as stamp duty for every state, borrowing power and the first home buyer grants calculator. The methodology and data sources pages say where the figures come from.
