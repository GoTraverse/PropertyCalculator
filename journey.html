What the First Home Journey is

Buying a first home in Australia is a six-month-plus process that most people walk blind: schemes they never heard about, budgets set in an auction room, deadlines tracked on a fridge calendar. The First Home Journey breaks it into seven stops, in the order the purchase actually happens — and at every stop, the numbers are computed for your situation, not a national average.

The seven stops

  1. Get your bearings — a two-minute wizard: where you're buying, what you earn, what you've saved, and where you are in the process. Every answer powers a real number later.
  2. Find your path — the scheme projector. The 5% Deposit Scheme, Help to Buy shared equity, and the no-scheme paths side by side: when you could buy, cash needed, LMI paid, repayment — with price caps and income caps checked against your answers.
  3. Set your real budget — every upfront cost including the solicitor bill that surprises everyone, a lender-style stress test at the 3% buffer, and one committed walk-away number.
  4. Pick your ground — suburbs whose verified sale medians actually fit your band, from licensed Valuer-General data, plus a search across 1,473 suburbs with current government figures.
  5. Hunt & compare — a library of every place you browse, inspect, bid on or lose at auction, each compared against your cap, with the honest note you'll trust later.
  6. Seal the deal — the clocks that start the day you sign: cooling-off, building & pest, finance approval, your pre-approval's own expiry, settlement. Each one counted down.
  7. Settle & move in — the final checklist through to the keys.

Frequently asked questions

Is the First Home Journey free?
Yes — every stop is free and nothing is locked behind a paywall. You don't need an account to start; a free account only adds saving across devices and sharing your journey with a partner.
Which government schemes does it compare?
The 5% Deposit Scheme (expanded First Home Guarantee), the federal Help to Buy shared-equity scheme, and your state's first-home stamp duty concessions — side by side with the no-scheme paths (saving to a 20% deposit, or buying sooner with a 10% deposit plus lenders mortgage insurance). Every figure is computed from your own numbers. Scheme eligibility should always be confirmed with the scheme administrator and your lender.
Where does the suburb data come from?
Licensed CC BY 4.0 state-government datasets: sale medians from the Valuer-General of Victoria and the Valuer-General of South Australia (metro Adelaide), and median rents from the Queensland RTA, SA Consumer & Business Services and Tasmanian rental bonds. Every figure carries its period and source, and rents are never presented as prices.
Do I have to start at the beginning?
No. The first question asks where you are in your journey — already inspecting homes, or a contract signed — and takes you straight to the stop that matches. The earlier stops stay open to go back over any time.
Can my partner or family follow along?
Yes. You can share a follow-along link (view-only, no account needed) or a partner link that lets the two of you work the same journey together, with changes syncing both ways.
Is this financial advice?
No. The journey is general information and education only — the formulas are shown, the assumptions are stated, and nothing here considers your personal circumstances. Talk to a licensed adviser, broker or your lender before acting.
