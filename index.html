Where the numbers come from

Stamp duty and first home concessions come from each state revenue office. Scheme settings come from Housing Australia, tax rates from the ATO and the 3% buffer from APRA. These are the FY2026-27 settings.

Suburb rents and sale medians are state government open data, shown with their period and source: Queensland, Victoria, South Australia and Tasmania. There are no investment scores or growth forecasts here.

The formulas are on the methodology page and each dataset is on the data sources page.

Who made this

I built this in 2026 while my partner and I were buying our first home. Stamp duty was on one site, an LMI estimate on another and the Queensland shared-equity scheme somewhere else, so I put the sums into a spreadsheet and later turned it into this site. There's more on the about page.

I'm not a financial adviser, and everything here is general information. If a figure or a rule looks wrong, tell me which page it's on and I'll check it against the source.
