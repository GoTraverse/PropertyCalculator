About EquitySight

I'm Jacoby. I built this site in early 2026 while buying my first home, and I run it on my own.

Why I made it

My partner and I started house-hunting in January 2026. Stamp duty was on one site, an LMI estimate on another and the Queensland shared-equity scheme somewhere else, so I put them into a spreadsheet. It grew to a dozen tabs. In February 2026 I turned it into this site.

What's on it

The main part is the First Home Journey, which takes a first purchase in seven steps in the order they happen. It starts with your savings and income and finishes at settlement. There are also 18 calculators for single questions like stamp duty or repayments.

It's free and you don't need an account. If you make one, your journey is saved to it and you can open it on another device.

Where the numbers come from

The calculators follow the published rules from the ATO, APRA, the RBA and the state revenue offices. Suburb pages use ABS 2021 Census population counts, a community postcode list cross-checked against Australia Post, and current rents and sale prices from state government open data. If I can't verify a figure, I take it off the site.

The methodology page has the repayment formula and says which suburb figures are measured data. The data sources page lists each dataset with its period and licence.

I draft the written guides with AI help, then review and edit them.

What it isn't

I'm not a financial adviser or a property professional, so nothing here is financial advice. Nothing on the site is paid for by a third party, and I don't sell your personal information. The Privacy Policy lists the services I use to run the site.
