How it started

The spreadsheet that became an app.

In 2023, trying to figure out whether the Queensland Shared Equity Scheme actually made sense for our budget, we built a spreadsheet. It had 14 tabs, nested formulas, and still couldn't tell us what we owed the government in year 10.

We started talking to other first home buyers and heard the same story over and over. The scheme is genuinely useful — it lets people who couldn't otherwise afford a deposit get into the market. But it's complex, and the complexity was stopping people from using it confidently.

"We wanted a tool that told you the full cost, the 30-year picture, and the break-even point — all from one place."

So we built EquitySight. It started as an internal tool, went through dozens of iterations shaped by feedback from real buyers, and is now available to anyone navigating the path to homeownership in Australia.

We're a small team — we answer support tickets ourselves, we ship updates based on real feature requests, and we care deeply about making this genuinely useful. Not startup-speak useful. Actually useful.

What we believe

Our values

🔍

Clarity over complexity

Property finance doesn't need to be opaque. Every number we show you has a reason, and every calculation is explainable in plain English.

🤝

Honest about limitations

We're a calculator, not a financial adviser. We're explicit about what our tool can and can't tell you — and we'll always point you to a licensed professional for the big decisions.

🏠

Built for buyers

Everything we build is shaped by real buyers in the middle of real decisions. We don't ship features we haven't tested with people actually buying property.

🔒

Your data, your business

We don't sell your data. We don't share your scenarios with third parties. What you model is private, full stop.

🚀

Ship and listen

We release updates every week. Most features were requested by users. We read every support email and reply within 24 hours.

🌏

Australian focused

We're built specifically for the Australian market — state-by-state scheme rules, LMI calculations, stamp duty by state, and suburb growth data from Australian sources.

Editorial standards

How we keep ourselves honest.

Every number on EquitySight is computed deterministically from published government data. Nothing is hand-written per suburb, nothing is generated by a chatbot, and nothing is paid for by a third party. We publish the exact formulas and the exact data sources so any reader can reproduce our output.

📐

Published methodology

Every score, strategy verdict and comparison delta is documented formula-by-formula. Read the EquitySight Methodology to see exactly how the numbers are computed, including the cash-flow coverage ratio, rate-sensitivity stress test and rental-stress threshold.

📚

Attributed data sources

The ABS 2021 Census, Australia Post postcode file, OpenStreetMap amenities and the RBA cash-rate feed — with licensing, refresh cadence and attribution — are listed on the Data Sources & Attribution page. We do not accept paid data from agents or developers.

✅

Corrections policy

If you spot an error on any suburb page or calculator, email support@equitysight.app. Confirmed corrections are logged publicly in the methodology change log, with the date and the field that changed — so you can see exactly what was fixed.
