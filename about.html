How it started

It began as a spreadsheet.

I started house-hunting in January 2026. Stamp duty was on one site, an LMI estimate on another, and the Queensland shared-equity scheme somewhere else. I built a spreadsheet to pull them together, and it grew to a dozen tabs.

In February 2026 I turned the spreadsheet into EquitySight. It started as a tool for my own purchase.

What it is now

The site is built around the First Home Journey. It breaks a first purchase into seven steps, in the order they happen. You set out your situation, compare the government schemes on your own numbers, and fix a budget with a maximum price. Then you shortlist suburbs, keep a record of the places you inspect, count down your contract deadlines, and work through settlement.

The 18 calculators sit beside the journey for single questions, like stamp duty or repayments.

It is free, and you don't need an account to start. An account saves your journey so you can open it on another device.

Accuracy

Where the numbers come from

The calculators follow the published rules from the ATO, APRA, the RBA and the state revenue offices. Suburb pages use ABS 2021 Census population counts, a community postcode list cross-checked against Australia Post, and current rents and sale prices from state government open data. When I find a figure I can't verify, I remove it.

The written guides are drafted with AI assistance, then I review and edit them. Nothing on the site is paid for by a third party. I don't sell your personal information. The Privacy Policy lists the service providers I use to run the site.
