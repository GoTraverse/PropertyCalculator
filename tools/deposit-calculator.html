What the calculator adds up

Cash needed up-front = deposit + stamp duty + legal and inspections + LMI. The loan is the price less the deposit.

  • Stamp duty is worked out from the price, the state and the first home buyer box.
  • LMI applies when the deposit is under 20%, which means the loan is more than 80% of the price (the LVR). At a 10% deposit the LVR is 90%, and at 5% it's 95%. The rates come from my rough table by LVR band.
  • Legal and inspections is a flat $2,500, my rough allowance for conveyancing plus building and pest inspections.

LMI premiums are set by the insurers (such as Helia or QBE) and passed on by the lender, so two banks can quote different figures on the same loan. Many lenders let you add LMI to the loan instead of paying it in cash, provided the total stays within their maximum LVR.

Under the 5% Deposit Scheme (formerly the First Home Guarantee), an eligible buyer pays no LMI, which would bring the 5% row down to $35,000. The calculator doesn't check scheme eligibility, so it always shows the LMI.

For a buyer who is not a first home buyer the calculator shows $22,275 of duty at this price. That is Queensland's standard rate. An owner-occupier pays less under the home concession, which the Queensland stamp duty calculator shows.

Schemes that lower the deposit

As at the 2026–27 financial year, the 5% Deposit Scheme (formerly the First Home Guarantee) lets an eligible first home buyer buy with a 5% deposit while the government guarantees the gap, so no LMI is charged. Since 1 October 2025 it has no income caps or place limits, but state price caps still apply.

Help to Buy is shared equity: the government takes a stake in the property in exchange for a smaller deposit and loan, and you buy its share back over time or when you sell.

Lenders also look for genuine savings, usually at least 5% of the price built up steadily over three or more months. Gifts and inheritances may not count on their own.

What the calculator leaves out

It doesn't test how much you can borrow, and it doesn't confirm that you qualify for any scheme. Loan application fees and moving costs aren't included. The cost of purchase calculator covers those. This is general information, not financial advice.
