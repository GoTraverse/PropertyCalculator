Australian property calculators

Where a calculator uses tax, duty or scheme rules, I've set them to FY2026-27 and the page names the source. If you're buying your first home, the First Home Journey takes you through the purchase in seven steps and uses the same stamp duty sums.

Buying a home

  • Stamp duty Duty in any state or territory, with first home buyer concessions and foreign buyer surcharges.
  • Cost of purchase What you pay on top of the deposit, such as duty, legal and bank fees, inspections and moving.
  • Deposit The cash you need at each deposit size once duty, LMI and legal costs are added.
  • First home buyer grants The grant and duty concession in your state, plus the federal schemes.
  • Listing price checker An asking price next to the government suburb median, with its period. Sale medians for Victoria and metro Adelaide, rents for QLD, SA and TAS.
  • Auction budget The most you can bid once duty and fees are paid, and a plan to print for auction day.

Stamp duty by state

Each state page uses the same duty schedule as the all-states calculator and explains that state's rules.

  • NSW stamp duty First home buyers pay no duty up to $800k.
  • VIC stamp duty First home buyers pay no duty up to $600k.
  • QLD stamp duty First home buyers pay no duty up to $700k.
  • WA stamp duty First home buyers pay no duty up to $600k.
  • SA stamp duty No duty for a first home buyer on a new home.
  • TAS stamp duty The first home buyer exemption ended in June 2026.
  • ACT stamp duty Buyers eligible for the Home Buyer Concession Scheme pay no duty at any price.
  • NT stamp duty No duty on a house-and-land package.

Borrowing and repayments

  • Borrowing power What a bank might lend one or two applicants, with income shaded by type and HECS counted.
  • Loan serviceability How much you could borrow from after-tax income, living costs and debts, tested at your rate plus 3%.
  • Mortgage repayments Weekly, fortnightly and monthly repayments, with extra repayments and an offset balance.
  • Interest only vs principal and interest Repayments and total interest for each, and how much repayments rise when the interest-only period ends.
  • Mortgage stress test Your repayment as a share of income at your current rate and at a higher test rate.

Investment property

  • Rental yield Gross and net yield from the price, rent, running costs and vacancy.
  • Property cash flow Weekly cost or income after tax, with negative gearing worked out at FY2026-27 rates. It can fill in the suburb median rent.
  • Capital gains tax Tax on a property sale with the 50% discount, at FY2026-27 marginal rates.
  • Land tax Annual land tax in each state and territory across several properties, with the trust, company and foreign owner rules.
  • Home equity How much you could borrow against your home at a lender's LVR limit.
  • House flip profit Before-tax profit, ROI and annualised return on a buy, renovate and sell plan.
  • Renovation cost A low-to-high budget from floor area, scope and finish. The rates are my own estimates.
