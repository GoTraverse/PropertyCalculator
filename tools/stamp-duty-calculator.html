How We Calculate Australian Stamp Duty

Stamp duty (also called transfer duty or conveyancing duty) is a state government tax charged when you buy property. Each Australian state calculates it differently using tiered bracket systems based on the property price, type, and buyer status.

State-by-State Stamp Duty Rates (2026)

Rates vary significantly between states. Here are the key thresholds:

  • NSW: Tiered rates from 1.25% to 7% on portions above $3M. First home buyers exempt up to $800,000
  • VIC: Tiered rates from 1.4% to 6.5%. First home buyers exempt up to $600,000, concessions to $750,000
  • QLD: Tiered rates from 1.0% to 5.75%. First home buyers get concessions up to $550,000
  • SA: Tiered rates from 1.0% to 5.5%. No stamp duty for first home buyers on properties up to $650,000
  • WA: Tiered rates from 1.9% to 5.15%. First home buyers exempt up to $430,000
  • TAS: Tiered rates from 1.75% to 4.5%. First home buyers get 50% discount up to $400,000
  • ACT: Tiered rates from 0.68% to 7.17%. First home buyers exempt up to $1,000,000
  • NT: Tiered rates starting at 0%. First home buyers get concessions up to $650,000

QLD Stamp Duty Calculator — Queensland Transfer Duty

In Queensland, stamp duty is officially called transfer duty and is administered by the Queensland Revenue Office (QRO). It applies to most property transactions and is calculated on the property's dutiable value (usually the purchase price).

QLD First Home Buyer Concession

Queensland first home buyers can receive a transfer duty concession administered by the QRO:

  • Up to $500,000: No transfer duty payable (full concession)
  • $500,001 – $550,000: Partial concession — duty reduces on a sliding scale
  • $550,001 – $800,000: Home concession rate applies (lower than standard rates)
  • Over $800,000: Standard transfer duty rates apply, no concession

The property must be your principal place of residence to qualify. Use the calculator above and tick "First home buyer" to see your exact QLD concession.

QLD Foreign Buyer Additional Duty

Foreign persons purchasing residential land in Queensland pay an additional 8% foreign acquirer duty on top of the standard transfer duty rate. Select "Foreign buyer" in the calculator to include this surcharge.

First Home Buyer Exemptions (All States)

Every Australian state offers some form of stamp duty relief for first home buyers. The exemption typically applies up to a price threshold, with partial concessions available above that. Our calculator automatically applies these when you check the "First home buyer" box.

Foreign Buyer Surcharges

Foreign buyers face additional stamp duty surcharges in most states, typically 7–8% on top of the standard rate. This calculator includes these surcharges when you check the "Foreign buyer" box.

Residential vs Vacant Land

Stamp duty rates often differ between residential dwellings and vacant land. Vacant land typically attracts lower duty in most states. Select your property type to see the correct calculation.
