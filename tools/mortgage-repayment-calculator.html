How Mortgage Repayments Are Calculated in Australia

Australian home loan repayments follow the standard amortisation formula: each repayment covers the interest accrued on the outstanding balance, plus a portion of principal. Early in the loan, most of your repayment is interest; late in the loan, most is principal.

Fortnightly repayments save thousands over the life of the loan because you end up making an extra month of repayments each year (26 fortnights × half a monthly repayment = 13 monthly repayments).

Principal + Interest vs Interest Only

Principal + Interest reduces your loan balance with every payment and is standard for owner-occupiers. Interest Only keeps the balance unchanged for a fixed period (typically 1–5 years), lowering short-term cash-flow but costing more over the life of the loan. Investors sometimes prefer interest-only for tax purposes.
