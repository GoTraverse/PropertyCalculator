Stamp duty rates in South Australia

RevenueSA charges stamp duty on conveyances under the Stamp Duties Act 1923. It’s worked out on the dutiable value, usually the contract price, across nine brackets. Owner-occupiers and investors pay the same rates, because SA has no general owner-occupier concession.

First home buyers in South Australia

Eligible first home buyers pay no stamp duty on a new home, an off-the-plan apartment, or vacant land they build a new home on. There’s no value cap for contracts entered into on or after 6 June 2024 (the old $650,000 and $700,000 thresholds no longer apply). You need to live in the home as your principal place of residence for at least six months in a row. On a new home or an off-the-plan apartment the six months have to start within 12 months of settlement. On vacant land they have to start within 12 months of the date the home can lawfully be lived in, or within 36 months of settlement if that comes first.

Established homes don’t qualify, so a first home buyer pays full duty on one. The First Home Owner Grant in SA is also limited to new builds. The rules are on RevenueSA’s first home owner page.

Registration fees

The Land Services SA transfer fee has no cap and rises with the price.

What this calculator leaves out

It can’t tell a new home from an established one, so a first home buyer sees standard duty. On a new home that qualifies, the duty is $0. Off-the-plan, pensioner and other concessions aren’t modelled either.

Frequently asked questions

When is stamp duty due in SA?

Within two months of the dutiable transaction, which is usually the contract date. Your conveyancer normally pays RevenueSA at settlement. Late payment attracts interest and penalty tax under the Taxation Administration Act 1996 (SA).

What is the SA foreign ownership surcharge?

A foreign person buying residential property in South Australia pays a 7% surcharge on top of standard duty, and RevenueSA collects it with the duty. Australian citizens and permanent residents aren’t foreign persons for this surcharge.
