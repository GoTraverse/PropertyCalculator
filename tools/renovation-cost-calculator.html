Key costs people forget to budget for

  • Council approvals / DA: $2,000–$15,000+ depending on scope
  • Structural engineer report: $1,500–$4,000
  • Asbestos testing / removal: Highly variable; required in pre-1990 homes
  • Temporary accommodation if you need to vacate during works
  • Holding costs: Mortgage + rates during the renovation period
  • Contingency: Always allow 10–20% above quoted costs

How to get the most accurate estimate

This tool gives you a ballpark for planning — to get a real number, obtain at least 3 quotes from licensed builders. Supply a detailed scope of works so you're comparing like for like.
