# ── Suburb pages ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# /suburb/ is crawlable. Only suburbs with current state-government market data
# (QLD, VIC, SA, TAS; about 1,475 pages) have a page at all, and those are the
# pages listed in sitemap-suburbs-*.xml. Suburbs without that data have no page.
#
# /invest/ holds the eight state directory pages. They carry "noindex, follow":
# they are navigation, not search results. They are left crawlable on purpose,
# because a crawler that is blocked from a page cannot read its noindex, and
# every suburb page's breadcrumb points at its state directory.
